Interactive introduction

What is
motion graphics?

Motion graphics extends graphic design into time. Graphic elements such as typography, images, logos and information are organised through movement to communicate.

In this study: motion graphics is considered as a time-based form of visual communication within multi-platform digital contexts.

Why does this matter
to my research?

Across these digital communication contexts, designers repeatedly make visual judgements over time. In this study, rhythm organisation, visual organisation and information clarity are treated as key factors in effective visual communication. These recurring demands provide the basis for the Dynamic Motion Communication Framework.
