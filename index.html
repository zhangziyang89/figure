What is
motion graphics?

Motion graphics is the design of graphic elements over time. Typography, shapes, images, logos and information can move to communicate, explain, identify and guide attention.

Why this matters to my research: contemporary digital communication increasingly requires designers to make visual decisions across time, movement and platforms.

One time-based language.

Across these contexts, motion graphics combines graphic design, animation, information design and audiovisual communication. Its distinctive variable is time, which makes rhythm, visual organisation and information clarity central to communication.
