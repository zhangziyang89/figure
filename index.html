What is
motion graphics?

Motion graphics is the design of graphic elements over time. Typography, shapes, images, logos and information are animated to communicate, explain, identify or guide attention.

The moving image on the right is an example of title-sequence motion design. It shows how typography and graphic elements can become part of time-based visual communication.

The common ground

Different fields.
One time-based language.

Across these applications, motion graphics combines graphic design, animation, information design and audiovisual communication. The defining addition is time: visual elements can appear, transform, move, sequence and respond.
