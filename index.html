What is
motion graphics?

Motion graphics is the design of graphic elements over time. Typography, shapes, images, logos and information can move to communicate, explain, identify and guide attention.

Research context: for contemporary digital communication, designers make visual decisions not only in space, but also across time, movement and platforms.

The common ground

Different applications.
One time-based language.

Across these contexts, motion graphics combines graphic design, animation, information design and audiovisual communication. Its distinctive variable is time, which makes rhythm organisation, visual organisation and information clarity important to visual communication.
