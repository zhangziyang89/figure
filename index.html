What is
motion graphics?

Motion graphics brings graphic design into time and space. Typography, shapes, images and sound can move to organise attention, communicate information and create meaning.

Step 03 · The idea

It is not one field.
It is an intersection.

Motion graphics sits between graphic design, animation, film, digital media and communication. What makes it distinctive is the use of time and movement as part of visual communication.
